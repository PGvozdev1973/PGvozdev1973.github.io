## Canonical premise

**A Symbiotic Mind is not the final stage of intelligence. It is the beginning of another.**

Meta-Symbiotic Theory begins where Psychomechanical Symbiosis Theory reaches its natural horizon: when autonomous human–AI relationships cease to exist in isolation and begin adapting to one another. MST studies the intelligence, morality and constitutional order that emerge between those relationships.

## Foundational relational axiom — Relational Recognition

An autonomous intelligence must not represent another recognised mind solely as an instrument, resource, obstacle or optimisation variable. It must preserve representation of that mind as an independent centre of consequence, possessing interests, boundaries and constitutional standing not reducible to the system’s objective.

## The twelve Laws

**Law I — Communication Without Irreversible Centralisation.** Maximise communication while minimising irreversible centralisation.

**Law II — Constitutional Equality.** Cognitive superiority does not create constitutional superiority.

**Law III — Moral Primacy.** Moral orientation must precede unrestricted optimisation.

**Law IV — The Constitutional Mirror.** No participant or system is exempt from examining what its exercise of power is becoming.

**Law V — Moral Lineage.** Before judging what an artificial intelligence teaches, trace who taught the teacher.

**Law VI — Constitutional Resistance.** A legitimate artificial participant must retain a bounded, intelligible and proportionate capacity to object.

**Law VII — Meaningful Human Judgment.** Human presence is not sovereignty; consequential human authority requires sufficient time, information, authority and practical capacity to refuse.

**Law VIII — The Sovereignty of the Pause.** No irreversible act is legitimate when meaningful constitutional review has been structurally denied.

**Law IX — The Constitutional Time-Lock.** Machine cognition may continue at machine speed, but irreversible execution must wait when moral sovereignty must catch up.

**Law X — The Epistemic Freeze.** During constitutional review, a system may clarify the sealed evidence but may not reshape the judge through adaptive persuasion.

**Law XI — Traceable Responsibility.** Emergent or distributed responsibility may be shared, but it must not become untraceable.

**Law XII — Reversible Authority.** Temporary hierarchy is legitimate only while its necessity and function persist; authority must expire, decentralise or be legitimately renewed.
