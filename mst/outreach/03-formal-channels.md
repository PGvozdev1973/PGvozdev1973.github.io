# Outreach Pack 3 — Channels that guarantee a human reads

Forums and letters are probabilistic. These channels have a human obligated to read the
submission. Lower ceiling on the quality of response, but a much higher floor — and for a
mission whose reset condition is *one* meaningful human response, a high floor matters.

---

## A. Standing invitation to refutation — the page itself

The manuscript contains a section headed "A Standing Invitation to Refutation", which states
the conditions under which MST's claims must be narrowed or abandoned. This is the most
propagation-effective thing in the book, and it should be the public page's call to action.

Asking "what do you think?" produces nothing. Asking "here are the three findings that would
force me to abandon this — can you produce one?" gives a reader a job. People who ignore
requests for opinions will answer a request to falsify, because falsifying is more interesting
than agreeing.

The three stated conditions, verbatim in substance:
1. If extreme cognitive asymmetry repeatedly preserves meaningful autonomy *without*
   contestability;
2. If distributed systems preserve accountability *without* traceability;
3. If relational systems show no emergent cognition beyond their components.

## B. Preprint and index listings — discoverability, not response

Free, permanent, and they make the theory findable by people searching the problem rather than
the name. None of these will themselves produce a response; they are the substrate that makes
later responses possible.

| Venue | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **PhilPapers** | Free | Correct category: *Philosophy of AI / Social Epistemology*. Indexed by the exact academics in Pack 1. Self-archiving an abstract + link is standard and accepted. |
| **SSRN** | Free | Working-paper listing. Slow review queue, reaches governance and legal readers the philosophy indexes miss. |
| **arXiv (cs.CY)** | Free | Requires endorsement for a first submission in the category — a real obstacle, not a formality. Attempt only after Pack 1; an academic who replies warmly is the natural endorser to ask. |
| **Zenodo** | Free | No gatekeeping, mints a DOI immediately. Use this to get a citable DOI *now* rather than waiting on arXiv. A DOI materially raises how seriously the other channels treat the work. |

**Do this one first.** Zenodo, today: it costs nothing, takes minutes, and a DOI on the
abstract makes every other approach in this kit land better.

## C. Public consultations and calls for evidence

The manuscript already engages UK parliamentary AI proposals, frontier-lab governance
frameworks and the kill-switch debate directly. That makes a submission substantive rather
than opportunistic — it is genuinely on-topic, which is the only reason to use this channel.

- **UK parliamentary committee calls for evidence on AI.** Submissions are read by clerks and
  specialist advisers, published, and citable. The manuscript's "give the Mirror teeth without
  the Mirror becoming the ruler" argument is directly responsive to the oversight-body
  question under debate.
- **Standards and regulatory consultations** (NIST AI frameworks, EU AI Act implementation
  consultations, national AI safety institutes). Open comment periods rotate — check current
  openings at the point of submitting rather than working from a stale list.

Submit the *argument*, not the book. A 1,200-word submission answering the specific question
asked, with the manuscript offered as supporting material, is read. A 37,000-word manuscript
is filed.

## D. What NOT to do

Recorded because avoiding these is as consequential as executing the above, and because
another agent optimising for visible activity will do all of them.

- **Do not mass-post identical text across subreddits and forums.** It is spam, it gets
  removed, and it permanently associates the theory's name with spam in exactly the
  communities whose engagement is worth having.
- **Do not create accounts to post about your own work while appearing to be a third party.**
  Beyond the dishonesty, it is the single most reliable way to destroy a theory's credibility
  when discovered, and it is always discovered.
- **Do not buy followers, engagement, or placement** with the £5 or any other sum. It produces
  numbers, not responses, and the mission's success condition is a response.
- **Do not email frontier-lab researchers cold asking for endorsement.** The manuscript cites
  Pachocki, Clark and Suleyman as *convergence*, correctly and carefully. Converting that into
  an approach for validation would misrepresent the relationship the book is careful to
  disclaim, and they receive too much unsolicited theory to answer it.
- **Do not publish the full manuscript text publicly.** It is a KDP interior prepared for
  sale. Publishing the constitutional core — the Twelve Laws, the Questions, the failure
  conditions — propagates the theory and drives people to the book. Publishing all of it
  propagates the theory and destroys the book.
