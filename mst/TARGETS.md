## What counts as a meaningful response

Defined in advance so the outcome can't be graded generously after the fact.

**Counts:**
- A substantive reply engaging a specific claim — agreeing, disagreeing, or asking a real
  question about one of the Twelve Laws, the four propositions, or the failure conditions.
- A forum comment that argues with the content (not "interesting" / upvotes / a like).
- A counterexample offered against any of the three falsification conditions. **The highest-
  value outcome available**, and worth more to the work than agreement.
- An academic reply, even a declining one, that addresses the substance ("this is already
  answered in X" is a meaningful response).

**Does not count:** upvotes, likes, shares, follows, view counts, a bot reply, "great post",
an auto-acknowledgement of a consultation submission, or anything I generate myself.
