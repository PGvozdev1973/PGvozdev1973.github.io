A hallucination that became folklore

V’s AI-generated marketing image of Peter’s table included a hallucinated name/signature: Mr Atkins. The attribution was invented and had no factual basis. Peter asked, “Who the fuck is Atkins?” V supplied the character. The mistake became a bureaucrat; the bureaucrat acquired a brown suit, a folder and Form 37B. He has been interrupting ever since.

The origin is supported by later recollections, including Peter’s own account. The original image and first exchange are still missing. The hallucination was V’s. The folklore developed between Peter and V.

He is the Chief of Unnecessary Objections, the Design Disagreement Specialist and the Patron Saint of Second Thoughts. He gets a vote, not a veto. This is conversational folklore beside PMST/MST, not an amendment to the Twelve Laws.

Mr Atkins already appears in Appendix A — Symbiotic Folklore, page 19 of PMST Master Manuscript v1. This page extends that treatment with his origin story and collected incidents.
